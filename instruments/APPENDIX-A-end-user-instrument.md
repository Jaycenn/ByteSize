# Appendix A — End-User Usability Instrument (Adapted PSSUQ, Version 3)

**Study:** Adaptive File Compression System Using Multi-Level Frequency Analysis & Hybrid Huffman ("ByteSize")
**Institution:** School of Computing, Holy Angel University
**Respondents:** End users (5–10 general computer users who regularly manage large files)
**Administration:** Google Forms, after the fixed task set
**Source instrument:** PSSUQ Version 3 (Lewis, 1992, 2002), contextualized to ByteSize

---

## Response scale

All rating items use a 7-point scale with a Not Applicable option:

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | N/A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|-----|
| Strongly Agree | Agree | Somewhat Agree | Neutral | Somewhat Disagree | Disagree | Strongly Disagree | Not Applicable |

The source instrument's polarity is retained, so **a lower score denotes better perceived usability.**
This direction must be stated wherever results are reported.

---

## Fixed task set (completed before the questionnaire)

1. Compress a file.
2. Verify the reported result.
3. Restore the original file.
4. Locate a previous item in the processing history.

---

## Part I — Adapted PSSUQ items (16)

### System Usefulness — items 1–6

1. Overall, I am satisfied with how easy it is to use ByteSize.
2. It was simple to compress a file and restore it again using ByteSize.
3. I was able to complete the assigned tasks quickly using ByteSize.
4. I felt comfortable using ByteSize.
5. It was easy to learn to use ByteSize.
6. I believe I could become productive quickly using ByteSize.

### Information Quality — items 7–12

7. ByteSize gave error messages that clearly told me how to fix problems (for example, an unsupported file, a file over the size limit, or a failed upload).
8. Whenever I made a mistake using ByteSize, I could recover easily and quickly.
9. The information ByteSize provided — on-screen instructions, labels, and the compression and decompression result summaries — was clear.
10. It was easy to find the information I needed, such as the original size, the compressed size, the compression ratio, and the space saved.
11. The information was effective in helping me complete the assigned compression, restoration, and processing-history tasks.
12. The organization of information on the ByteSize screens was clear.

### Interface Quality — items 13–15

13. The interface of ByteSize was pleasant.
14. I liked using the interface of ByteSize.
15. ByteSize has all the functions and capabilities I expect it to have.

### Overall — item 16

16. Overall, I am satisfied with ByteSize.

## Part II — ByteSize-specific functional feedback (8)

Reported separately. **Never combined into a construct score.**

17. I am confident that the file ByteSize restored is identical to the original file I compressed.
18. The difference between the Fast, Balanced, and Maximum compression presets was clear to me.
19. The space saving that ByteSize reported after compression was easy to understand.
20. The processing history made it easy to review the files I had already compressed or restored.
21. It was easy to manage my stored compressed results, including downloading a result again or removing one.
22. The analytics and report views gave me a useful summary of my compression activity.
23. Having separate pages for compression and decompression made the system easier to use, not harder.
24. I would be willing to use ByteSize on my own files.

## Part III — Open-ended feedback (3)

Reported separately, thematically.

25. Which features of ByteSize did you find most useful, and why?
26. What difficulties, confusing parts, or errors did you encounter while using ByteSize?
27. What further suggestions do you have for improving ByteSize?

## Scoring

| Score | Items | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **System Usefulness (SYSUSE)** | 1–6 | Arithmetic mean |
| **Information Quality (INFOQUAL)** | 7–12 | Arithmetic mean |
| **Interface Quality (INTERQUAL)** | 13–15 | Arithmetic mean |
| **Overall** | 1–16 | Arithmetic mean across all 16 items |

- Item 16 is a summary satisfaction statement. It contributes to the **Overall score only** and belongs to no construct.
- All scores are arithmetic means of their constituent items.
- **Not Applicable responses are omitted, not imputed.** Compute each mean over the items actually answered.
- Lower scores indicate better perceived usability.

### Interpretation constraint

Because the items are contextualized rather than reproduced verbatim, this instrument is an **adaptation** of the PSSUQ and not the PSSUQ itself. Scores are interpreted **within this study** and are **not** compared against the normative values published for the original instrument, since those norms describe responses to the original wording.

With 5–10 end users, results support a **formative** usability assessment reported with descriptive statistics. This is not sufficient for inferential comparison against PSSUQ norms, and no such comparison is claimed.
